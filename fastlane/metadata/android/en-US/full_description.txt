The Bible Verse app offers daily inspirational Bible verses and verse image artwork in multiple languages and translations.

Features:
- Daily Verse of the Day & Verse Images
- Home screen widgets (Text Verse Widget & Daily Verse Image Widget)
- Dynamic language and translation selection
- Daily customizable notifications
- Easy sharing with friends and family

Powered by the YouVersion Platform SDK. Verse content and artwork provided by YouVersion / Life.Church.

The app is open-source and published on GitHub: https://github.com/joshuabeny1999/BibleVerseApp
